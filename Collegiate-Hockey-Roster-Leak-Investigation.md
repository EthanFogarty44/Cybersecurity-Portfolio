# Collegiate Hockey Roster Leak Investigation

## Overview

Following the completion of team tryouts involving 40 high school players, the finalized roster was intended to remain confidential until its official release the following week.

Several days before the scheduled release, an anonymous Instagram account published a post identifying eight players who had reportedly made the team and thirteen players who had not been selected for the final roster. Because the information was not yet public, concerns arose that roster information had been improperly accessed or disclosed.

An investigation was initiated to determine how the information was obtained and whether available digital evidence could identify the source of the leak.
## Scenario

A high school hockey organization completed a three-day tryout process involving 40 student-athletes competing for roster positions. Following the completion of evaluations, the Head Coach and Assistant Coach used organization-provided email accounts to discuss player selections. Once finalized, the roster announcement was distributed to media interns responsible for creating the public announcement and was scheduled for publication on the organization's Instagram account the following Monday using the platform's post-scheduling feature.

Access to roster information was limited to coaching staff and designated media interns involved in preparing the official announcement.

Three days before the scheduled release, an anonymous Instagram account published a graphic identifying eight players who had reportedly secured positions on the team. The post also identified thirteen players who had not been selected for the final roster. The information appeared to match details contained within the scheduled roster announcement and internal team communications.

Because the information had not yet been publicly released, team management raised concerns that confidential roster information may have been improperly accessed, shared, or disclosed. A digital forensic investigation was authorized to determine how the information may have been obtained and whether available evidence could identify the source of the leak. The investigation was initiated due to concerns regarding email security, account access controls, and the security of the organization's social media management processes.
## Investigative Scope


This investigation was initiated after confidential roster information appeared on an anonymous Instagram account prior to the scheduled public announcement. The primary objective is to determine how the information was accessed and identify the path through which the confidential information was disclosed.

Investigators are authorized to examine organization-controlled evidence, including email communications, access records, social media management records, and other relevant digital artifacts. The evidence collected during the investigation will be used to establish a timeline of events, determine who had access to the roster information, and evaluate whether any activity can be reasonably associated with the unauthorized post.

Because the incident involves high school student-athletes, ethical considerations remain a priority throughout the investigation. All findings must be supported by evidence and documented objectively. The investigation must protect the privacy of students and staff while remaining focused on determining how the information was disclosed.
## Evidence Examined

Investigators were provided with the following evidence:

- Email communications between the Head Coach and Assistant Coach regarding roster selections and announcement planning.
- Email records showing distribution of the finalized roster announcement to media interns responsible for preparing social media content.
- Copies of group message communications exchanged between coaching staff, recruiting personnel, and media interns containing roster-related information.
- Access records associated with organization email accounts involved in roster planning and distribution.
- Login activity associated with the organization's Instagram account and related social media management platforms.
- Screenshots of the leaked Instagram post published by the anonymous account.
- The original roster announcement graphic prepared for publication.
- Metadata associated with the roster graphic and related announcement materials.
- A list of individuals authorized to access roster information.
- Workstation activity records from devices used to create, edit, or distribute announcement materials.
- Records associated with the scheduled Instagram post, including creation, viewing, and modification activity.
- Internal communications related to roster decisions and announcement preparation.
